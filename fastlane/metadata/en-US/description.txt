Tawwab (تَوَّاب) is a completely free Islamic companion app — no ads, no payments, no subscriptions.

FEATURES:
• Prayer Times — Accurate prayer times calculated from your location using the Egyptian General Authority of Survey method.
• Quran — All 114 surahs with authentic Uthmani text, searchable and easy to read.
• Global Tasbeeh — A digital tasbeeh counter with haptic feedback. Track your dhikr count and see your global ranking.
• Azkar — Morning, evening, post-prayer, and sleep adhkar from Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim).
• Dhambi (My Sin) — A unique repentance companion. Select a sin and receive guided steps for sincere tawbah, expiation (kaffarah), and correcting the rights of others.

Tawwab means "The Accepter of Repentance" — one of the beautiful names of Allah. This app is built purely for good deeds (hasanat) and is 100% free forever.
