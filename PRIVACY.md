# Privacy Policy — Tawwab (تَوَّاب)

Last updated: November 20, 2026

## Overview

Tawwab is a free Islamic companion app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## Data We Collect

### We do NOT collect:
- Your name, email, or phone number
- Your personal identity
- Any payment information (the app has no payments)
- Photos or media from your device

### What we use locally (on your device only):
- **Location**: Used solely to calculate accurate prayer times. We use your approximate location to determine prayer timings. This data never leaves your device.
- **Tasbeeh count**: Your dhikr count is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. It is not sent to any server.
- **App preferences**: Your selected dhikr type and settings are stored locally.

### Network access:
- The app fetches Quran text from the public API `api.alquran.cloud` to display Quranic verses.
- No personal data is transmitted in these requests.

## Third-Party Services

- **api.alquran.cloud**: Public Quran text API. No personal data is sent.
- **Apple/Google services**: Standard platform services for app distribution only.

## Children's Privacy

Tawwab is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Any changes will be posted in this file.

## Conclusion

Tawwab is built for hasanat (good deeds) only. Your privacy is sacred to us.
